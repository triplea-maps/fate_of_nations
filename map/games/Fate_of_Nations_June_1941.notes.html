FATE OF NATIONS: June 1941

The war has been raging for nearly two years, but in June 1941 Germany prepares its most audacious plan yet: Operation Barbarossa, the largest land invasion in history. Across Europe, Africa, and Asia, the Axis and Allied powers now face decisions that will determine the fate of nations and whether the war follows its historical course—or takes an entirely different path.

Fate of Nations expands the traditional TripleA/A&A game into a larger-scale World War II strategy game map emphasizing logistics, infrastructure, economics, and political decisions.

1. GAME SETUP

Fate of Nations begins in June 1941.

The major powers and minor powers are placed according to the June 1941 scenario setup.

Bidding

Bidding is available as a game option using the standard TripleA bidding system.

Game Options

  • Triggered Victory
  • Total Victory
  • Projection of Power
  • Politics
  • Low Luck
  • Low Luck for Anti-Aircraft
  • Low Luck for Technology
  • Low Luck for Bombing and Territory Damage
  • Other TripleA game options shown during setup

Victory thresholds can be configured when the corresponding victory option is enabled.

2. BARBAROSSA

Germany begins the game at war with the Soviet Union.

Before the normal German turn sequence begins, Germany receives a one-time Barbarossa opening phase representing the German operational surprise of June 1941.

  1. German Barbarossa Combat Move
  2. German Barbarossa Battle
  3. German Barbarossa Non-Combat Move
  4. German Barbarossa End Turn

The normal game sequence then begins.

The Barbarossa opening is a one-time game opening and does not repeat later in the game.

3. PLAYERS AND TURN SEQUENCE

Major and Minor Powers

Fate of Nations distinguishes between Major Powers and Minor Powers.

Some Minor Powers operate within the turn of an associated Major Power. Their purchases and placements are generally conducted during that Major Power's turn.

Once the applicable political conditions are met, the associated Major Power may control the Minor Power's units as described in Section 4 — Political Relationships.

4. POLITICAL RELATIONSHIPS

Fate of Nations uses an expanded political system.

Countries may have relationships including:

  • War
  • Allied
  • Cooperative
  • Neutral

Germany

Germany begins at war with the UK and USSR. Germany may declare war on the USA at any time.

Italy

Italy begins at war with the UK and USSR. Italy may declare war on the USA at any time.

Japan

Japan begins at war with China.

Japan may declare war on:

  • UK
  • USSR
  • USA

at any time.

USSR

The USSR may declare war on Japan at any time.

United Kingdom

The UK may declare war on Japan at any time.

United States

The USA may declare war on an Axis power:

  • on or after Round 5;
  • if an Axis power enters a territory in North or South America;
  • if an Axis power declares war on the USA, allowing the USA to declare war on other Axis powers;
  • if London or Moscow falls;
  • or against Japan if Japan first declares war on the UK.

The USA does not receive this last option if the UK declares war on Japan first.

Brazil

Brazil does not collect normal income until it enters the war.

Brazil enters the war and begins collecting its normal income when the USA is at war with all three Axis powers.

Mexico and much of Latin America provide economic support to the USA even without direct military involvement.

Mongolia

Mongolia does not collect normal income until it enters the war.

Mongolia joins the USSR when the USSR and Japan are at war and begins collecting its normal income at that time.

USSR–Western Allies Cooperation

The USSR is Cooperative with the Western Allies.

Soviet/Mongolian units cannot enter Western Allied territories, and Western Allied units cannot enter Soviet/Mongolian territories, unless Moscow falls.

Soviet/Mongolian units cannot enter Chinese territories, and Chinese units cannot enter Soviet/Mongolian territories, unless Moscow falls or both the Soviet Union and China are at war with Japan.

Lend-Lease is an exception to these restrictions.

6. VICHY FRANCE AND FRENCH TERRITORIES

Vichy France begins the game as an Axis aligned power and controls its metropolitan territories and colonies. Its land units are limited to Vichy territories and its warships have anchored in Vichy Ports in a strictly defensive posture, although transports may move between the Vichy territories. Most of the Vichy French economic benefit of its territores in Europe and Africa are transferred to Germany while the economic benefit of French Indochina is transferred to Japan.

Vichy West Africa Breakaway

If Dakar, Morocco, or Algiers is no longer controlled by Vichy France, the designated French West African territories break away from Vichy control and become Free French.

Operation Torch / Case Anton

If the Allies' Operation Torch is successful and they take control of Morocco or Algiers, Germany will take control of Vichy France's metropolitan French and North African territories and the military assets in those territories, while Japan takes control of Vichy French Indochina territories.

Vichy Naval Forces

Vichy naval units affected by the transfer have a 50% chance of switching allegiance to Germany and a 50% chance of being scuttled.

7. NEUTRAL POWERS

Spain, Sweden, and Turkey

Spain, Sweden, and Turkey begin the game neutral.

Declaring war on one of these powers requires a political action and a cost of 25 IPU's. When war is declared, the neutral power joins the opposing side.

Other Neutral Territories

Other neutral territories follow the traditional neutral-territory rules, except that invading a neutral territory carries a cost of 10 IPU's.

8. LOGISTICS AND TRANSPORTATION

Fate of Nations includes several transportation systems for moving military forces and equipment.

Rail

Cost: 3 IPC

  • Required for Train movement.
  • Represented by a physical unit on the map.
  • Can be damaged and repaired.
  • Can be targeted by strategic bombing.
  • Can sustain up to 2 damage.
  • Becomes inoperable when damaged.
  • Suffers maximum damage when captured.

Train

Cost: 12 IPC    Movement: 4

  • Requires Rail to move.
  • Receives a movement bonus of 1 if movement starts at a factory or industrial complex.
  • Cannot make a Combat Move.

Transport Capacity:

  • 4 Infantry/Marines/Paratroopers; OR
  • 2 Trucks/Artillery/Mechanized Infantry/Tanks; OR
  • 2 Infantry/Marines/Paratroopers and 1 Truck/Artillery/Mechanized Infantry/Tank; OR
  • 3 Lend-Lease Units; OR
  • 2 Lend-Lease Unit and 1 Infantry/Marines/Paratroopers; OR
  • 1 Lend-Lease Unit and 2 Infantry/Marines/Paratroopers; OR
  • 1 Lend-Lease Unit and 1 Truck/Artillery/Mechanized Infantry/Tank.

Truck

Cost: 3 IPC    Movement: 2

  • Provides local land transportation.
  • Can transport infantry, marine, paratrooper, or Lend-Lease unit.
  • May deliver transported units into battle during Combat Movement.
  • Attacks at 0.
  • Defends at 0.

Air Transport

Cost: 9 IPC    Movement: 4
Attack: 0    Defense: 0

Capacity: 1 Paratrooper, Infantry, or Marine.

  • Requires an appropriate Airfield and industrial facility for production.
  • Paratroopers dropped from an Air Transport attack at 2 during an airborne assault.

9. INDUSTRAL PRODUCTION

Industrial Complexes, Factories, Armories, and Barracks provide the infrastructure to produce units.

All can be damaged and are valid targets for strategic bombing.

Industrial Complex

Production Capacity: 15

  • Germany, USSR, Japan, USA, and Great Britain each have an Industrial Complex.
  • Cannot be built or replaced during the game.
  • Can sustain up to 30 damage.
  • Each damage point reduces production capacity by 1.
  • Converts to a Factory and suffers maximum damage when captured.

Factory

Production Capacity: 6

Cost: 15

  • Factories may only be built in originally owned territories with a value of 2 or more and must replace an existing Armory.
  • Can sustain up to 12 damage.
  • Each damage point reduces production capacity by 1.
  • Suffers maximum damage when captured.

Armory

Production Capacity: 3

Cost: 15

  • Armories may be placed in territories with a value of 2 or more.
  • Armories' production is limited to: infantry, trucks, artilley, and mechanized infantry.
  • Can sustain up to 6 damage.
  • Each damage point reduces production capacity by 1.
  • Suffers maximum damage when captured.

Barracks

Production Capacity: 3 Infantry/Marines/Paratroopers

  • Placement restricted to a country's original territories.
  • Barracks' production is limited to: infantry, marines, and paratroopers.
  • Can sustain up to 6 damage.
  • Each damage point reduces production capacity by 1.
  • Destroyed when captured by an opposing power.

10. AIRFIELDS AND HARBORS

Airfields and Harbors provide infrastructure for air and naval operations.

Both can be damaged and are valid targets for strategic bombing.

Both can sustain up to 6 damage and become inoperable when they sustain more than 3 damage.

Harbors

  • Sea units require a Harbor and Factory/Industrial Complex for production.
  • Provide a movement bonus of 2 for Convoy Transports.
  • Provide a movement bonus of 1 for all other sea units.
  • Can repair damaged Carriers and Battleships.

Airfields

  • Air units require an Airfield and Factory/Industrial Complex for production.
  • Provide a movement bonus of 1 to all air units.
  • Up to 3 Fighters may scramble from an Airfield to adjacent territories.

15. NATIONAL UNITS

Many countries have national versions of standard units.

National units generally retain:

  • their national name;
  • national artwork;
  • the characteristics of the corresponding standard unit.

They are treated as the corresponding standard unit unless their rules specifically differ.

National units may also be transferred to the control of another Major Power under the Minor Powers and Unit Control rules.

17. VICTORY

Fate of Nations uses an expanded Victory City system with Victory Cities throughout the world.

Victory may be determined by several configurable systems.

Triggered Victory — Default Setting

Axis Victory

The Axis achieves victory by satisfying one of the following:

  • Capturing the three major Allied capitals — London, Moscow, and Washington D.C. — while retaining control of two of the three major Axis capitals — Berlin, Rome, and Tokyo;
  • Capturing two of the three major Allied capitals and controlling 26 Victory Cities while retaining control of two of the three major Axis capitals;
  • Capturing one of the three major Allied capitals and controlling 36 Victory Cities while retaining control of two of the three major Axis capitals.

Allied Victory

The Allies achieve victory by satisfying one of the following:

  • Capturing the three major Axis capitals — Berlin, Rome, and Tokyo — while retaining control of two of the three major Allied capitals — London, Moscow, and Washington D.C.;
  • Capturing Berlin and Rome and controlling 15 Victory Cities in the Pacific/Asia Theater while retaining control of two of the three major Allied capitals;
  • Capturing Tokyo and either Berlin or Rome and controlling 28 Victory Cities in the Atlantic/Europe/Middle East/Africa Theater while retaining control of two of the three major Allied capitals.

Total Victory — Optional

Total Victory can be enabled as a game option.

Default thresholds:

  • Axis: 50 Victory Cities
  • Allies: 50 Victory Cities

The required number of Victory Cities is configurable in the Game Options.

Projection of Power — Optional

Projection of Power can be enabled as a game option.

Default thresholds:

  • Axis: 30 Victory Cities
  • Allies: 40 Victory Cities

The required number of Victory Cities is configurable in the Game Options.
